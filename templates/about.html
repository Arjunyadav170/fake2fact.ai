About Fake2Fact

Fake2Fact is an AI-powered fact-checking tool designed to help users quickly verify the accuracy of information they come across online. Whether it’s a claim on social media, a news article, or a trending rumor, Fake2Fact empowers you to separate fact from fiction

Why Fake2Fact? (Problem Statement)

Misinformation spreads faster than ever in today’s digital world. False or misleading claims can influence opinions, harm public health, or create confusion. Fake2Fact aims to counter this by providing users with AI-assisted verification that is fast, transparent, and easy to use

Global Overview of Misinformation: Countries ranked by prevalence of misinformation.

How It Works

1. Input a Claim or URL: Enter a statement or paste an article link.
2. Retrieve Evidence: Our system searches reliable sources and fact-checking databases.
3. Analyze with AI: A large language model (LLM) processes the claim, compares it with retrieved evidence, and provides an explanation.
4. Get a Verdict: Results are shown as True, False, or Misleading with confidence scores.

Key Features

  • Text-based claim verification
  • URL verification for online articles
  • 🚧 Image & Audio verification (coming soon)
  • 📊 Confidence score with explanation

    • Disclaimer

    Fake2Fact uses AI and external data sources to assist in fact-checking. While it provides useful insights, it may not always be 100% accurate. Users are encouraged to consult official fact-checking organizations for final confirmation.
